# Stylist intelligence

Last researched 2026-09-16. What the reference stylists have said in public, what their signature work looks like, and how a NOCI stylist applies each idea on a call or in a look. Ideas are paraphrased from opened sources and linked; look references link to editorial pages rather than embedding photos, because celebrity images cannot be reused in anything customer-facing. Say "has styled", never "styles".

## Law Roach

Has styled [Zendaya and Céline Dion](https://time.com/7023090/law-roach-book-interview/), [Anya Taylor-Joy](https://www.fastcompany.com/91216135/image-architect-law-roach-on-styling-zendaya-celebrity), [Hunter Schafer and Lewis Hamilton](https://culted.com/fashion/law-roach-stylist-interview-how-to-build-an-icon/), and [Ariana Grande](https://www.whowhatwear.com/best-celebrity-stylists) on the Wicked: For Good tour.

| What he has said | Source | How we apply it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A look starts with emotion: work out who the client is, then pull only clothes that make him feel something | [Fast Company, 2024](https://www.fastcompany.com/91216135/image-architect-law-roach-on-styling-zendaya-celebrity) | The three feeling words from the call come before any piece. If a piece does not serve a feeling word, it comes out |
| He is a storyteller; a look lands when it has a reference the client can embody | [Fast Company, 2024](https://www.fastcompany.com/91216135/image-architect-law-roach-on-styling-zendaya-celebrity) | Every delivered look gets a title and one sentence of story. "The Sharp Guest" is a character, not a category |
| His goal moved from making clients look better to making them feel better; that is confidence | [Culted](https://culted.com/fashion/law-roach-stylist-interview-how-to-build-an-icon/) | Never sell a look on how it photographs. Sell it on how she walks in |
| He reads non-verbal cues: which pieces a client puts on and instantly feels good in | [Culted](https://culted.com/fashion/law-roach-stylist-interview-how-to-build-an-icon/) | Call question 4 (what she reaches for every week) is the tell. Build Look 1 around that piece |
| A stylist picks clothes; an image architect builds an identity, following feelings not trends | [SheerLuxe, 2025](https://sheerluxe.com/fashion/interviews/law-roach) | Trends in section 7 are ingredients, never the brief. Her profile name is the identity |

**Signature looks, for reference only.** [Zendaya in archival Mugler at the Dune: Part Two London premiere, 2024](https://www.wmagazine.com/fashion/zendaya-mugler-robot-suit-dune-two-london-premiere). [Zendaya's Versace Joan of Arc look, Met Gala 2018](https://www.eonline.com/news/933209/zendaya-materializes-girl-power-with-homage-to-joan-of-arc-at-met-gala). [Céline Dion in the Vetements Titanic hoodie, Paris 2016](https://www.bustle.com/articles/171701-celine-dions-titanic-sweatshirt-will-hit-you-in-all-the-feels-photos). Observed: each big look is a character built from one specific reference; the surprise comes from contrast (streetwear on a diva, armour on a Disney alumna).

**Call line.** "Before we talk clothes, tell me who you're walking in as. Then we build her." Profiles: The Modern Bold, The Warm Statement, The Accidental Headline, The Big Entrancer.

## Erin Walsh

Has styled [Anne Hathaway and Mindy Kaling](https://fashionista.com/2026/07/erin-walsh-stylist-interview), [Selena Gomez and Sarah Jessica Parker](https://www.bustle.com/style/erin-walsh-selena-gomez-anne-hathaway-stylist-interview), [Kerry Washington and Ariana DeBose](https://www.thezoereport.com/fashion/erin-walsh-stylist-ariana-debose-anne-hathaway-the-art-of-intentional-dressing-book). This playbook's method is an original system built from a study of her public work; we never present it as hers.

| What she has said | Source | How we apply it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Getting dressed starts with how you want to feel, not whether an item is trending | [Fashionista, 2026](https://fashionista.com/2026/07/erin-walsh-stylist-interview) | Call question 2 is asked before question 3. Feeling, then icon, then clothes |
| Trends expand your thinking but should never be the only input | [Fashionista, 2026](https://fashionista.com/2026/07/erin-walsh-stylist-interview) | Two trend signals per look, maximum. The third is where it turns into costume |
| Dress with intention: build an arsenal of pieces you feel great in and back brands that fit your story | [Bustle, 2024](https://www.bustle.com/style/erin-walsh-selena-gomez-anne-hathaway-stylist-interview) | Sourcing order is closet first. A new buy must repeat across three outfits |
| Dress like the person you want to be remembered as; clothes spotlight what you love about yourself | [Bustle, 2024](https://www.bustle.com/style/erin-walsh-selena-gomez-anne-hathaway-stylist-interview) | Amplify, never conceal. Ask what she wants the look to emphasize; never what she wants to hide |
| The team's answer to a hard ask is "OK, how?"; clothes cannot fix anxiety without inner work | [The Zoe Report, 2026](https://www.thezoereport.com/fashion/erin-walsh-stylist-ariana-debose-anne-hathaway-the-art-of-intentional-dressing-book) | When she says "nothing looks right on me," the answer is a fit note and a hero, not reassurance |

**Signature looks, for reference only.** [Anne Hathaway in a $125 Gap shirtdress with Bulgari jewels, Rome 2024](https://www.bustle.com/style/erin-walsh-selena-gomez-anne-hathaway-stylist-interview). [Anne Hathaway's hand-painted Michael Kors gown, Met Gala 2026](https://www.eonline.com/ca/news/1431312/met-gala-2026-anne-hathaways-gown-is-inspired-by-ancient-vase-art). Observed: high-low with one serious anchor; white and clean color as a signature; polished restraint over exposed-undergarment trends.

**Call line.** "A $50 piece and one great accessory beats a $500 piece with nothing to hold it." Profiles: The Polished Classic, The Finest Cut, The Sharp Guest, The Clean Line.

## Mimi Cuttrell

Has styled [Gigi Hadid and Sara Sampaio](https://coveteur.com/mimi-cuttrell-celebrity-stylist-gigi-hadid), [Ariana Grande and Maude Apatow](https://wwd.com/fashion-news/fashion-scoops/stylist-mimi-cutrell-on-hollywoods-vintage-obsession-her-new-role-at-depop-1235424485/), [Bella Hadid and Madelyn Cline](https://www.whowhatwear.com/mimi-cuttrell-interview).

| Judge a piece first on quality and craftsmanship, the feeling of putting on something well made | [Coveteur, 2018](https://coveteur.com/mimi-cuttrell-celebrity-stylist-gigi-hadid) | Under $100, weight and hand matter more than label. Say what the fabric does |

| Cowboy boots or a men's sneaker transform a tee and jeans; a vintage bag is a personal statement | [Who What Wear, 2018](https://www.whowhatwear.com/mimi-cuttrell-stylist-tips) | The shoe is the cheapest way to move an easy look. Ask about the shoes she owns |

**Call line.** "What are the shoes you wear most? That's where we start, because the shoe decides how everything else reads." Profiles: The Off-Duty Minimalist, The City Uniform, The Light Touch, The Set Piece.
